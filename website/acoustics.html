What is exact here?

The visible mesh is the assembled, validated production STL. Its triangle surfaces are sampled onto the solver grid and form the solid boundary for pressure and all three velocity components.

Read the model honestly

01

Production solid boundary

The three validated print STLs are assembled at their production offsets. Their sampled triangle surfaces—not duplicated tube dimensions—define the 1 mm no-slip CFD boundary.

02

Exterior compressible LES

The solver advances all three velocity components and acoustic pressure through the bore and exterior air. A Smagorinsky subgrid viscosity dissipates unresolved turbulent eddies.

03

Resolved scale and limits

This is a browser-scale 1 mm large-eddy simulation, not micrometre direct numerical simulation. The mouth jet and exterior radiation are simulated, while eddies below one voxel are modeled.
